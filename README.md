#  Password Manager

A secure, offline password manager built with **wxWidgets** — because the safest place for your passwords is your own computer.

---

## What It Does

Your personal vault for storing passwords safely on your local machine. No cloud, no leaks, no worries.

For each password entry, you can store:
- **Email / Username**
- **Password**
- **Website URL**
- **Notes** (anything you want to remember about the site)

You can **add**, **edit**, or **delete** entries anytime.

---

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Categories** | Organize passwords into custom categories (e.g., Work, Personal, Finance) |
| **PIN Lock** | Secure the app with a PIN — required every time you open it |
| **Edit Entries** | Update your saved passwords, URLs, or notes whenever needed |
| **Password Generator** | Create strong passwords with your preferred length and character types |
| **Strength Checker** | Instantly see how strong your password is |
| **Smart Search** | Find passwords by searching through notes, email/username, or category |
| **PIN Recovery** | Forgot your PIN? Recover it using your custom security question & answer |
| **Auto-Lock** | App automatically locks if left idle for over 5 minutes |

---

## How to Run

### First-Time Setup
1. **Launch** the program by double-clicking its icon.
2. **Set your PIN** — this will be required every time you open the app.
3. **Set a Security Question & Answer** — you'll need this if you ever forget your PIN.
4. You're all set!

### Daily Use
1. **Open the app** and enter your PIN.
2. **Add a password** by clicking the **Add Password** icon.
3. **Organize with categories** — click **Categories** to create or manage groups.
4. **Search, edit, or delete** entries as needed.

---

## Quick Tips

- **Create categories first** to keep things tidy from the start.
- **Use the password generator** for strong, unique passwords.
- **Check password strength** before saving — weak passwords are flagged automatically.
- **Don't forget your security Q&A** — it's your only way back in if you lose your PIN!

---

## Why Offline?

Your passwords never leave your computer. No servers, no syncing, no third-party access — just you and your data.
